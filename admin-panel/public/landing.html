Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Splenexa?

Explore how Splenexa's FMGE learning and practice tools fit into your preparation routine.

What is Splenexa?

Splenexa is an AI-powered FMGE preparation app with a 19-subject Q Bank, PYQ-based questions, a test series, video lectures and an AI Medical Dictionary.

What is FMGE?

The Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) is a screening test conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) for people with a medical degree from outside India who want to practise in India.

Who is Splenexa for?

Splenexa is built for FMGE aspirants, both first-attempt students and repeaters. It works on Android and iOS.

What does the Q Bank include?

The Q Bank covers 19 subjects with textbook-verified explanations and PYQ-based questions. It is enhanced for 3 month preparation and has no timer.

What is in the Test Series? Is there negative marking?

The Test Series includes subject wise tests, mock tests, grand tests and image based tests. Tests are timed with fixed question sets, there is no negative marking, and there is a leaderboard.

What is the AI Medical Dictionary?

It explains medical terms, definitions and concepts in simple language, with AI-powered explanations that give FMGE medical context.

How much does Splenexa cost?

Foundation costs ₹499 for 3 months, ₹999 for 6 months and ₹1999 for 12 months. Elevation costs ₹999 for 3 months, ₹1999 for 6 months and ₹3999 for 12 months.

What do the Foundation and Elevation plans include?

Foundation includes the Q Bank and Test Series. Elevation includes Videos, the Q Bank and the Test Series.

Does Splenexa guarantee that I will pass the FMGE?

No. Splenexa provides preparation resources and does not guarantee FMGE qualification or any particular examination result.
